For the best result

  • Photograph one leaf, filling most of the frame.
  • Use daylight; avoid harsh shadows and flash glare.
  • Put the leaf on a plain background if you can.
  • Include the affected part — spots, mould, curling.
  • If the confidence is low, retake from another angle.

The model recognises 15 crop–condition classes from PlantVillage (bell pepper, potato and tomato). Other crops will not be recognised reliably.
