How Whale Optimization tuned the model

A whale swarm searches the space of feature extractors + classifiers + hyper-parameters. Each candidate is scored by fitness = validation accuracy − λ·latency/budget − λ·model size/budget, so the winner is accurate and cheap enough for a field device.
